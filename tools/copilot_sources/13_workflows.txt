## How a Workflow runs

Each job has a notebook, admin/workflows/<name>/<name>.ipynb. The user fills in its names
cell and saves it; then `project.check()` names the next step of each Workflow started, and
`project.do_next()` runs the steps it can, up to the first it can't. A step that is a call
to the warehouse or a decision is the user's: give its cell, never run it for them. Names in
capitals, such as NAME and TABLES, are the names cell's. DEV and PROD, the Dev and Prod
Databases, are set once in admin/databases.py.
